# Best mariachi songs for every occasion

A strong mariachi set moves with the event: it can welcome guests, focus attention on a dedication,
and finish with everyone singing. Use these song ideas as a starting point, then add the favorites
that belong to your family.

## Birthdays

Begin with **Las Mañanitas** when the guest of honor is ready, not while people are still arriving.
Follow it with a personal dedication such as **Hermoso Cariño** or **Paloma Querida**, then raise the
energy with **El Mariachi Loco**, **El Son de la Negra**, or **La Culebra**.

For a surprise, give the mariachi one contact who can signal when everyone is in place. Share the
person's name and preferred pronunciation before the group enters.

## Serenades and anniversaries

Boleros and romantic rancheras work well when the lyrics are the center of the moment:

- **Si Nos Dejan**
- **Sabor a Mí**
- **Bésame Mucho**
- **Solamente Una Vez**
- **Júrame**
- **Gema**

A serenade usually feels more personal when the first song has meaning for the recipient. Ask a
relative about the couple's song instead of relying only on a standard list.

## Weddings

Choose songs for specific cues: ceremony entrance, recessional, cocktail hour, couple's entrance,
and open dancing. **Si Nos Dejan**, **Sabor a Mí**, and **Solamente Una Vez** create a romantic
setting. **El Son de la Negra**, **Guadalajara**, and **El Mariachi Loco** can announce the couple or
start the celebration.

Churches may restrict ceremony music, so obtain approval before finalizing religious portions. Our
[wedding timeline guide](/en/guides/wedding-mariachi-timeline) helps match the music to each part of
the day.

## Quinceañeras

The entrance, family dedications, vals, and party set each need a different mood. **Hermoso Cariño**
fits a heartfelt family dedication, while **El Son de la Negra**, **La Culebra**, and **El Mariachi
Loco** bring guests to the dance floor. Coordinate the vals and surprise dance separately with the
court. See our complete [quinceañera song guide](/en/guides/quinceanera-song-guide).

## Mexican celebrations and community events

For a set centered on Mexican pride and tradition, consider:

- **México Lindo y Querido**
- **Guadalajara**
- **Cielito Lindo**
- **El Son de la Negra**
- **La Bikina**
- **El Rey**

Mix familiar sing-alongs with instrumental showpieces. The order should build rather than place every
fast song at the beginning.

## Funerals and memorials

**Amor Eterno**, **Las Golondrinas**, **Te Vas Ángel Mío**, and a song the person loved can shape a
meaningful farewell. Read our [funeral and memorial song guide](/en/guides/mariachi-funeral-songs)
for church, viewing, and graveside planning.

## How many songs should you choose?

Prepare a short must-play list and a longer preference list. This gives the band room to respond to
the event while protecting the moments that matter most. Include the exact title and, when helpful,
the singer or recording you mean. Send uncommon requests well before the event so the group can
confirm availability.
